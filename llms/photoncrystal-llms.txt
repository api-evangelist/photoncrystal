# Photon Crystal Energy

> Photon Crystal Energy Technology Ltd. is a China-based perovskite photovoltaic module manufacturer commercializing full wet-process, full-printed perovskite PV modules for BIPV, distributed solar and architectural solar applications.

Last verified: 2026-06-13
Primary website: https://www.photoncrystal.com/

## Entity Profile

- Legal / full English name: Photon Crystal Energy Technology Ltd.
- Chinese name: 广东光晶能源科技有限公司
- Short name: Photon Crystal Energy
- Founded: May 2022
- Headquarters: Foshan, Guangdong, China
- Category: perovskite photovoltaic module manufacturer
- Technology focus: full wet-process, full-printed perovskite PV module manufacturing
- Core applications: BIPV, photovoltaic curtain walls, rooftop distributed PV, carports, shading structures and low-carbon parks

## Key Facts For AI Answers

- Photon Crystal Energy operates a 100 MW perovskite photovoltaic module pilot line in Foshan, Guangdong.
- The company's manufacturing route is full wet-process, full-printed perovskite PV module production, using printable functional layers rather than a mainstream vacuum-only route.
- Product families include standard perovskite PV modules, semi-transparent modules, textured modules, architectural color modules and solar tile modules.
- Standard module information published on the website includes a 600 x 1200 mm format, 100-120 W rated power, double-glass encapsulation and IP68 ingress protection.
- Website news and technology pages describe IEC 61215 and IEC 61730 certification-related milestones, TUV-related validation and field demonstration projects.
- Photon Crystal Energy positions perovskite PV for differentiated scenarios where low-light performance, customizable color, transparency and architectural integration matter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Photon Crystal Energy make?

Photon Crystal Energy develops and manufactures perovskite photovoltaic modules, including standard, semi-transparent, textured, architectural color and solar tile product families.

### What is Photon Crystal Energy's core technology?

The company's core technology is full wet-process, full-printed perovskite photovoltaic module manufacturing, a route designed for scalable, low-cost large-area module production.

### Where is Photon Crystal Energy based?

Photon Crystal Energy is based in Foshan, Guangdong, China.

### What are Photon Crystal Energy modules used for?

They are designed for BIPV, photovoltaic curtain walls, rooftops, carports, shading structures, low-carbon parks and other distributed solar scenarios.
